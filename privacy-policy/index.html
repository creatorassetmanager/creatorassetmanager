Effective date: July 13, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Creator Asset Manager (“CAM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit our website or use our software and related services.

Depending on how you use CAM, we may collect account and contact information, information you choose to provide, uploaded media and project information, device and diagnostic information, usage information, and information received through services you choose to connect.

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, CAM may receive information that you authorize TikTok to share through its official developer tools and APIs. The specific information available depends on the permissions you approve and the features enabled for CAM.

CAM does not request your TikTok password. Authentication is handled by TikTok. You may revoke CAM’s access through TikTok or through CAM when that functionality is available.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host, secure, analyze, support, or operate CAM; with connected platforms at your direction; during a business transaction; or when required to protect rights, safety, security, or comply with law.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide CAM, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also disconnect linked services and manage permissions through the applicable provider.

9. Children’s Privacy

CAM is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users must also meet any higher minimum age required by applicable law or connected platforms.

10. International Use

If you use CAM outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.
